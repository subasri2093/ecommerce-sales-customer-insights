🛡️ PII Masking & Data Privacy Safeguards

GDPR & HR Anonymization Protocol

To protect employee privacy and prevent identification, personal identifiable information (PII) is automatically redacted or hashed prior to analytics reporting.

  • Name, Email, Phone & SSN: Redacted/Stripped from reporting data.
  • Employee ID: Cryptographically hashed using salted SHA-256 algorithm.
  • Age Binning: Continuous ages grouped into 10-year brackets.
